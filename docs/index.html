Your projects

Your film stays yours.

Every film is a project on your Mac, with its drafts, its notes and its export in one place.

  • A folder for every film. Projects live in a local Documents/RasanAI library. Search, rename, archive or duplicate drafts. Files you add are copied in, and your originals are not changed.
  • Every version kept. Your notes and each version of the film are kept, and the final screen has a version picker.
  • Export from Studio. Play the finished film in the app, then export it. If something is off, say so and RasanAI makes the change.

Questions

Before you download.

What do I need to run it?

An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or newer.

Does it cost anything?

RasanAI Studio is free and open source under the MIT license. It uses your own Claude Code or OpenAI Codex, installed and signed in, so usage counts toward your own plan.

Is it finished?

No. It is a community beta. The real-agent generation flow is still in beta and not fully verified, so expect rough edges. Presenter films, where you film yourself on a green screen, are newer still and have experimental limits: read them in the field guide. Please tell us what breaks.

What happens the first time I open it?

This beta is ad-hoc signed and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm. Drag RasanAI Studio into Applications, right-click it and choose Open, or use Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. After that, signed updates arrive on their own. Full installation help.

What about rendering tools?

Use Help → Prepare Rendering Tools in the app. It installs whatever is missing.
