New film

Start with
a sentence.

One prompt box. Say what the film is about, and add whatever you have: footage, images, a brief, a brand guide.

  • Attach your material. Clips, stills and documents sit in the box with your words and become part of the story.
  • Choose a length and a shape. Chips along the bottom of the box set the running time and Landscape, Portrait or Square.
  • Set motion, brand and models. Motion at Maximal, Balanced or Minimal, a brand kit, and which Claude models direct it.

Brands

Your brand,
kept as a file.

A brand is a folder with a DESIGN.md: colours, type, logo. Pick one, and every film is drawn in it.

It is plain text, so you can edit it anywhere, keep it in version control, and hand it to a teammate.

Footage reels

Reels from
your footage.

Point it at a folder of clips. RasanAI cuts them into a reel and works motion into every part of it.

  • OverlaysTitles and callouts that sit on the footage, timed to it.
  • Framed shotsClips placed in frames, devices and layouts, not just full screen.
  • Caption boxesWords that arrive with the speech and stay readable.
  • Motion graphicsCards and moves between clips, turned up as far as you like.

Presenter films

You on a green screen,
in a world made for the words.

Film yourself talking in front of a green or blue screen. RasanAI keys you out, cuts your speech into beats and builds a world behind you for each one, with motion graphics that land on the right word. The pictures are made through Codex on your own ChatGPT plan, about a minute and a half each, and without Codex Claude draws the backdrops instead. It is new: checked on synthetic footage, not yet recorded on a real shoot, and the clip plays whole for now. Your answers to a few taste questions (plain, literal pictures or bold ones; a speaker who stays put; how much writing; captions; a badge to keep; an end card) are enforced by the checks, not guessed.

What you need

  • An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or newer.
  • Claude Code or OpenAI Codex, installed and signed in. Usage counts toward your own plan.
  • Rendering tools: use Help → Prepare Rendering Tools in the app to install what is missing.

The first time you open it

This beta is ad-hoc signed and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS will ask you to confirm.

  1. Drag RasanAI Studio into Applications.
  2. Right-click the app and choose Open.
  3. Or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

After that, signed updates arrive on their own, checked every 5 hours.

A community beta, honestly. The app is new and the real-agent generation flow is still in beta and not fully verified. Expect rough edges, and please tell us about them.
