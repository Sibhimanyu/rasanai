# Squeak brand assets

The duck, its moods (`duck-*.webp`), the duck mark (`squeak-duck.svg`), the horizontal lockup (`squeak-lockup.svg`) and the
wordmark (`squeak-wordmark.svg`, cut from the lockup) come from [Squeak](https://github.com/Sibhimanyu/squeak)
(`website/assets/`), licensed under the Apache License 2.0. They are used here, unchanged apart from the wordmark crop,
to show RasanAI working with Squeak.
