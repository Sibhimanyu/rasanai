Rare tools and AI clichés

No glow pulses, no bokeh

Never pulse a glow or float particles as atmosphere. Use real material or moving colour light.

Takeaways

  • No pulsing glow rings. No bokeh or particles.
  • Use real material, or colour light that moves.
  • No idle loops on content.

What it means

Pulsing rings, breathing glows, bokeh dots and floating particles are the clearest sign of a generated film. They are banned with no allowed use. A bloom tied to a word's meaning, real material, or colour light that actually moves does the same job honestly.

In a RasanAI film

This is one of two true invariants. cliche-glow-particles and cliche-banned-move flag it, and idle loops on content such as breathing, bobbing or an infinite yoyo are rejected, which also keeps every frame seek-safe for rendering.

Origins and sources

Engine invariant, from real launch films. Also a photosensitivity safeguard: see the accessibility laws. Community critics also single out deep shadows and glows that overpower the interface; that is one commenter's opinion, not studio consensus.

Evidence: Engine invariant, plus a community opinion.

  • Community critique of overly dark shadows and glows, r/MotionDesign, 26 April 2026 (opinion)
