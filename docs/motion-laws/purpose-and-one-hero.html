Motion

Purpose, and one hero

Every move has a job, and each beat has one hero motion.

Takeaways

  • Ask what each move tells the viewer.
  • One hero per shot; the rest support it.
  • Quiet things stay quiet, so the hero reads.

What it means

Motion that does not direct the eye, show cause and effect or carry a thought is noise. In each shot one thing is the hero, a second supports it a few frames behind, and everything else stays quiet.

In a RasanAI film

Every tween is scored with a layer (hero, support or ambient). The hero travels the furthest and fastest; support moves less and arrives later. Decorative loops on content are rejected.

Origins and sources

Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Motion: use motion purposefully and avoid gratuitous animation. Disney's staging principle: one idea at a time. Read the source.
