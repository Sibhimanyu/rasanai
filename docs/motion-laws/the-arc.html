Story

The arc

Every launch film runs hook, turn, meet, capabilities, payoff.

Takeaways

  • One thought per beat, with on-screen type carrying the story.
  • The hook is the viewer's problem, with no logo and no product name.
  • The meet is small and calm, on an empty ground.
  • Two or three capabilities, each proving one thing.

What it means

The order is fixed and the content changes every time. Each beat does one job, so a viewer who has never heard of the product can follow it with the sound off: a problem they feel, a pivot, the reveal, two or three proofs, and a close.

In a RasanAI film

The score declares a role for every beat (hook, turn, meet, capability, payoff) and the motion follows the role: tight and urgent in the hook, one sharp move on the turn, the longest and calmest entrance on the meet, the longest holds at the payoff.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
