Type and image

Editorial still life, no generated people

Use real-looking objects in natural light, and keep generated people out.

Takeaways

  • Objects, props, printed matter, materials.
  • Natural light, a photographic or print feel.
  • People only when the brief asks.
  • Review every image before it is used.

What it means

Generated faces and figures read as AI at a glance, and a blue-glow tech look reads as a template. Props, places, paper and print in natural light feel editorial and tactile, and they work well as content inside cards and collages.

In a RasanAI film

Image prompts ask for still-life objects in natural light, with no text and no people. The check imagery-people-or-glow flags a brief that asks for faces, hands or a neon look; people appear only when the brief asks for them.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
