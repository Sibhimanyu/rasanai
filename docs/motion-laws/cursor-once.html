Rare tools and AI clichés

One cursor, with a reason

Use a travelling cursor once per film, for a real action in the product.

Takeaways

  • Once per film.
  • Only for a real product action.
  • An arc with ease in and out, never linear.
  • State the reason in the score.

What it means

A cursor pointing at words is the standard AI emphasis. A cursor that clicks Create in a real prompt box is a moment of the product itself. Used once, with a reason, it is a tool; used across beats, it is a tic.

In a RasanAI film

A film may carry one travelling cursor, with a declared cursor_reason such as "clicks Create in the real prompt box". It is 3 to 4 percent of the short side, travels on an arc with ease in and out, never linear, and ends in one purposeful action.

Origins and sources

Engine rule from real launch films.
