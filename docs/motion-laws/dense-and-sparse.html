Scenes and rhythm

Dense, then sparse

Follow a crowded moment with an empty one, and never run more than two alike.

Takeaways

  • Alternate on purpose.
  • No more than two dense or two sparse in a row.
  • Scale contrast of at least 2.5 times.

What it means

Contrast is what makes each scene read. A collage lands because the single line before it was quiet; the quiet line lands because of the collage. Three dense beats in a row, or three sparse, flatten into a texture.

In a RasanAI film

Every beat declares its density. The gate warns on a run of more than two dense beats, and the biggest element in a dense beat should be at least 2.5 times different in height from the sparse one beside it.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
