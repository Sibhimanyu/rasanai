Motion

One signature curve

Put most smooth moves on one easing curve so the film has a voice.

Takeaways

  • Declare it once, in the score.
  • 70 to 85 percent of smooth moves.
  • Other curves are accents, not a menu.

What it means

A single curve used on most moves is what makes a continuous take feel designed. A few other curves are kept for contrast: a whip, a transition, the closing hit.

In a RasanAI film

A launch score declares one signature_ease, a hard launch with a long glide. The gate expects 70 to 85 percent of smooth moves on it and warns below 60 percent. Stepped beats set their own timing.

Origins and sources

Brand motion systems commonly use one easing for most moves; the exact curve and share here are measured from real launch films.
