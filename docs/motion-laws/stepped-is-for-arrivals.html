Motion

Stepped for arrivals, fluid for glides

Hold frames on discrete pop-ins; never on a smooth single-direction move.

Takeaways

  • Stepped: pop-ins, assembly, hits on the beat.
  • Fluid: scrolls, pans, pushes, glides, orbits.
  • Mix them inside one beat if you like; just not on one object.

What it means

Stepped motion, on twos or threes, gives pieces that assemble or jump a graphic rhythm. Applied to a scroll, pan or camera push it just looks like dropped frames. So the cards pop in stepped while the strip beneath them scrolls fluid.

In a RasanAI film

A beat marked stepped is checked against its named moves. If it contains a scroll, pan, push, glide or orbit, the gate warns. The usual fix is to step only the pop-ins and leave the scroll smooth.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
