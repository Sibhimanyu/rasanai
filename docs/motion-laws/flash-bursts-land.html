Scenes and rhythm

Flash bursts land on a readable frame

Flash one varying element, then land on a held frame that says something.

Takeaways

  • One varying element, locked layout.
  • 2 to 3 frames each; 8 to 24 frames in all.
  • Land on a held frame, 12 or more.
  • No information inside the burst.

What it means

A flash-frame burst builds anticipation before a reveal or a drop, or conveys speed and overload. Nobody can read information at two frames each, so the burst carries no message. The message waits on the frame it lands on.

In a RasanAI film

Vary one element in a locked layout, hold each flash for two or three frames, run the whole burst for 8 to 24 frames, then land on a frame held for at least 12 on the downbeat. One or two bursts per film, never back to back.

Origins and sources

Engine rule from real launch films, bounded by WCAG 2.3.1 (three flashes or below threshold). Read the source.
