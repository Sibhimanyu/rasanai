Type and image

Real UI as proof

Show the product doing its job, big enough to read.

Takeaways

  • 35 to 60 percent of the frame, as a starting range.
  • The product doing the job, not just present.
  • By about 40 percent of the runtime.
  • Rebuild the operation; leave out incidental handling.

What it means

A film that shows the product late, or never doing the job, is a mood piece. A proof card should take a real share of the frame, cropped or tilted if you like. Under about a quarter of the frame it is a chip or an accent, not a proof.

In a RasanAI film

UI is usually a simplified recreation that follows the story rather than a raw screen recording, sized at about 35 to 60 percent of the frame by area as a starting range. The first proof arrives by about 40 percent of the runtime, and at least one beat is marked core_job_demo. Training or evidence footage may still need the original capture.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law. Studios rebuild interface work around the narrative rather than filming raw capture.

Evidence: Studio practice.

  • MHF Creative, Awin brand film: rebuilt search, filtering and campaign workflows
  • Ordinary Folk, Webflow Ecommerce: the real UI stays focal
