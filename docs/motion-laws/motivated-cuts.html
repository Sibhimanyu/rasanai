Scenes and rhythm

Motivated transitions, cuts on the beat

Every transition has a reason, and hard cuts land on the music.

Takeaways

  • Carry velocity or an object across the seam.
  • Cut on a hit, a drop or a break edge.
  • A fade is not a transition.

What it means

A transition that is only decoration is a gimmick. A good one carries something across the seam: an object, a shape, a velocity. Picture on the beat feels intentional, so seams, hard cuts and the turn are aligned to the music's hits and drops.

In a RasanAI film

Seams come from a varied vocabulary (whip, push, mask wipe, scale-as-transition, carrier handoff), and the turn is placed on the drop in the music map. A plain fade, or blur with nothing travelling, does not count as choreography.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
