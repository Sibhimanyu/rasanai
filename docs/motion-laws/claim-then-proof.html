Story

Claim, then proof

Every claim is followed by the product doing it.

Takeaways

  • One line, one proof, side by side or back to back.
  • The proof is the product doing the job, not decoration.
  • Social proof is real or absent.

What it means

A line is a promise; the product doing the job is the evidence. A capability beat pairs one short line with one simplified screen that proves it, so the viewer does not have to take our word for it.

In a RasanAI film

The first UI proof lands by about 40 percent of the runtime, and at least one beat shows the core job being done. Numbers and claims come from research/claims.json, so an invented claim cannot reach the screen.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
