Scenes and rhythm

Scene variety

Give every scene its own idea, layout and ground.

Takeaways

  • Each scene has its own idea.
  • No move in more than two beats.
  • Vary scale, ground and texture together.

What it means

The same layout for forty-five seconds reads as a slide deck, however well each slide moves. Variety is the backbone: full-frame type, a UI proof, a collage, an object, a quiet line. Each scene brings its own idea, so no single gimmick carries the film.

In a RasanAI film

Beats declare a scene_type, and the gates warn when one carrier covers more than 40 percent of beats, a move repeats in more than two, or one transition family covers more than 30 percent of seams.

Origins and sources

RasanAI launch grammar, studied from real launch films. An engine rule, not an academic law.
