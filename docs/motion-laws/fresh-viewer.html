Story

The fresh viewer decides

Ask someone who has never seen it what the film was; their recall overrules the internal score.

Takeaways

  • Show it once, cold, with no explanation first.
  • Record what they recall unprompted.
  • The moments they could not follow are the edit list.
  • A specialist audience still needs a representative reviewer.

What it means

By the time a film is finished everyone who made it knows what it means. A person seeing it cold finds out whether the story survived. Their unaided answer to what the product is and why it matters is evidence. A model's or a designer's rating of its own work is not, and the double-read check a designer runs on their own copy is unreliable for the same reason.

In a RasanAI film

Before a film ships, an unfamiliar viewer watches once and answers without help: what is the product, what does it do for me, what did I miss. The unaided recall, and the moments they could not follow, can send the film back to the edit even when every internal gate passed.

Origins and sources

A practitioner working method: use people unfamiliar with the process for first reactions. The principle, not a metric: no source validates a recall score.

Evidence: Single practitioner.
