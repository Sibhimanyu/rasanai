# Call 4: music, animatic and build (direct path vs long path)

> Moved verbatim from the former single `SKILL.md`. References such as "§3d", "rule 18", "below" and "above" point into the sibling guides: the five-call contract and the hard rules are in [shared-contract](shared-contract.md); the phase guides are listed in `SKILL.md`.

## 4 · Animatic (the whole film, rough, with sound)

The animatic is the film before animation: every scene's key frame at its real duration, with the real music bed and the voice, so pacing, story and sound are judged together. This is where the user shapes the film.

**A lyric video builds its animatic from the plates, against the real track** (read this, then skip the numbered steps it replaces): scenes come from the chosen treatment, the music is the track whole and untouched (never `music.mjs` or `sound.mjs fit`: a song is not cut to a film; the plates are cut to the song), and there is no voice.

```bash
node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/treatment.mjs scenes --treatment "$RUN/story/chosen-treatment.json" --lyrics "$RUN/music/lyrics.json" --out "$RUN/scenes.json" [--aspect 16:9] [--music "<mood>"]   # one scene per plate; durations are the plate windows; each scene carries plate, idiom, space, energy, start, end, lines (indices into lyrics.json), motifs, ground
ffmpeg -y -i <track> -ar 48000 "$RUN/bed.wav"      # the bed: the whole track, as is; the workflow's own step makes assets/bgm.mp3 from it
```

`scenes.mjs` does not take this route (the workflow plans cuts from the beat grid); `treatment.mjs scenes` replaces it. Then the motion score (step 4 below) with the Motion Director's lyric-video rules: **spine = the style bible's signal; motifs = the treatment's; energy, space and durations are fixed by the plates** (`crew.mjs check` warns when the score contradicts a plate's space or energy); **hook plates are scored as one escalating set**, the whole curve first. Key frames (5), the gate (6) and the push (7) are as written; the animatic's `scenes` are the plates, `music` is the track, `voice` omitted. Notes that change a plate's idea go back to its idea in `chosen-treatment.json` (re-run `treatment.mjs check` and `scenes`); notes on timing snap to downbeats.

1. **Scenes from the chosen script** (craft, `references/craft.md`): build `$RUN/scenes.json` (format: `references/video.md`) from the picked story's beats: one scene per beat (split a long beat into shots if it needs them), its `on_screen`, `visual`, `vo` as the voiceover `line` and `duration_s`, keeping the approved words; only timing changes later (the music fit). Per scene `id`, `title`, on-screen text, `visual` (what we see), voiceover `line`, `duration`, `type`/`persuasion`/`beat` (launch films), `intensity`, `transition_in`. One job per scene; hook in the first 1.5–2 s in outcome language; text held at least 0.4 s per word + 0.6 s; no scene still for more than ~1 s; layouts vary; end card 1.5–3 s. Then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/scenes.mjs --scenes "$RUN/scenes.json" --route <route> --out "$RUN/plan"` (writes STORYBOARD.md, SCRIPT.md, timeline.json in the workflow's format; exit 1 lists problems: fix and re-run).
2. **Music** (`references/sound.md`): candidates long enough for the film, in the chosen film's feel, each analysed and fitted, then an edit that never loops audibly:

   ```bash
   node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/music.mjs --run "$RUN" --film <seconds> --style "<the style's words>" --story "<angle and tone>" --format launch|explainer|reel|kinetic|brand|premium [--narrated]
   node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/sound.mjs fit --track <picked file> --scenes "$RUN/scenes.json" [--reveal <s>] [--logo <s>] --out "$RUN/music/plan.json" --write-scenes "$RUN/scenes.json"
   node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/sound.mjs render --plan "$RUN/music/plan.json" --out "$RUN/bed.wav"
   ```

   The music follows the story's arc (`references/sound.md`, "The music arc"): the Motion Director writes `music_arc: { intro_until, drop_at, break, payoff_at, button_at }` in the score and aligns the reveal with `drop_at` and the logo with `button_at`; pass the reveal's and the logo's start times from the script to `sound.mjs fit` as `--reveal <s>` and `--logo <s>` (and re-run it with the score's times if they moved). Better music: the source chain is the user's own track (`--track <file|folder> --library <name>`; an Epidemic file is refused, use Epidemic Connect), then a track generated to the arc when the user has their own ElevenLabs or Stable Audio key (`RASANAI_ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` / `RASANAI_STABILITY_API_KEY`, pass `--arc "$RUN/motion/score.json"`), then the curated catalog, HeyGen, ccMixter and Openverse last; never a borrowed key.

   `music.mjs` writes mood intents as genre, instruments, tempo and structure (cliché words like "uplifting corporate" are stripped), keeps only tracks at least the film + 4 s that pass the measured quality gate (sub bass, harshness, bandwidth, width, punch, loudness range), and returns three options of different moods with `preview` (the fitted edit), `bpm`, `ending`, `fit`, `score`, `license` and a credit (licences go to `$RUN/music/LICENSES.json`, the credit to `CREDITS.txt` and `description.txt`: after the pick run `music.mjs credits --run "$RUN" --pick <id>` and put `description.txt` into the final's description; Mixkit `suggestions` are fetched only on `--pick`). Pick the one that best serves the film. The user's own track → `sound.mjs analyze` + `fit` on it. `fit` starts on a strong phrase, lands on the track's own ending (flexing the film ≤ ±1 s), snaps scene starts to bars, moves the reveal to a section start and the logo to the ending hit, and writes the new durations into scenes.json: re-run `scenes.mjs`. `needs_longer: true` → another candidate; never ship a loop. `render` masters the bed to −14 LUFS. Push `music` done ("<title>: one bar-line edit into its own ending, the reveal on the lift").
3. **Voice** (narrated films): `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/voice.mjs --run "$RUN" --line "<the hook line>" --tone "<2-3 words>"` gives samples; pick the one that fits the film and push `voice` done. The voice is the most-criticised part of AI video: calm, human pacing, no hype words; when in doubt, fewer lines. Narration itself goes through `voice.mjs speak --run "$RUN" --script <lines>` (take, transcribe, retake). A user's own speech with rushes: `footage.mjs clean-speech --in <clip>`. `unavailable` → say so; the workflow uses its default.
3a. **Check the voice against its script** (narrated films; before the user hears the animatic): `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/voice.mjs verify --audio <vo.wav> --script <lines.json> --names "<Brand>,<Product>" --project videos/<name> --run "$RUN"` (`references/deliver.md`). A line over 0.2 word error rate or a misheard name is an error (exit 2): re-record the line or respell the name for the TTS only (keep the caption right); a caption shorter than max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s) is an error too. After the final render, check the real mix: `voice.mjs verify --mix <final.mp4> --script <lines> --meta audio_meta.json --scenes scenes.json`. Text lands 0-2 frames before the spoken word (5 at most for a name reveal): run `voice.mjs refine --audio <clip> --words <words.json>` first and mark such events `word`. Exit 1 = it could not run (no whisper-cli): it logs a downgrade; tell the user the voice went unchecked.
3b. **Reference moments** (route `product-launch-video`, `faceless-explainer`, `pr-to-video`, `general-video`; crew phase `moments`): real motion from real launch films, as measured specs and code. The user's picked moves win (`moments: [{id, place?}]` in `rasanai-brief.json`, up to 4; `place` is `opening|between|peak|ending|anywhere`, default `anywhere`; a legacy `role` maps hook to opening, cta to ending, the rest to anywhere). Picks are role-free: there are no hook/proof/turn/cta slots, because the picks happen before the story exists. Once the story is chosen, the Motion Director places each pick on the beat whose mechanic fits best (respecting `place`) and records `moments_placed: [{id, beat, take, why}]` in score.json (`crew.mjs check` warns `moment-unplaced` for a pick no beat uses, unless `moments_dropped` gives a reason). When the user picked none, pick 2 to 4 by mechanic fit to the chosen story's beats: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/moments.mjs search --mechanic <tag> [--place <place>] [--query "<words>"]` (the manifest's role tags stay as search hints; `--role` still filters) (the public manifest, fetched at run time and cached under `~/.rasanai/cache/moments/`), then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/moments.mjs fetch <id> --run "$RUN" [--place <place>]` for each (sha256-checked, read-only under `$RUN/references/moments/<id>/`, the creator credited in `$RUN/references/moments/credits.json`). For beats no pick covers, `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/moments.mjs precedent --beat <hook|turn|proof|cta> --content <ui|type|logo|number|3d|abstract|photo> [--q "<words>"]` gives offline hints from human designers' rules (motionmaxxing, Apache-2.0; labelled "model-decoded precedent, unverified: a hint, not a rule"): take the rule and why, never above a measured number or the user's verdict. The Motion Director writes each beat's `moment` and `take` (one mechanic as one sentence: `references/craft.md`, the take rule) and the builder builds it fresh. **Technique only:** no footage, frames, code, copy, brand or audio from a moment ships in the film; nothing is vendored into the skill. Offline (`--no-network`) uses the cache; with none, the library's own motion styles (`library.mjs search --type motion`), and say so. Name each placement in the `motion` decision when you push it ("Your 'Cursor clicks' move opens beat 1: its click-to-zoom builds the first ask"). At the styleframes stage the designer proposes each pick's beat in `frames/shots.json` (`moment: {id, why}`); the user can Swap it in the console (`swap-moment`).
4. **The motion score** (the Motion Director; `agents/motion-director.md`). Once scenes.json has its final durations (after the music fit), re-plan with the scene count (`crew.mjs plan … --scenes <N>`) and dispatch `motion-director` with key `score`. The Motion Director reads `story/intent.json` first and chooses motion per beat from its `buys`: the hero is what must be noticed to get it, sized by importance; the verb comes from the meaning; the transition from the relation word. It spends 2 to 4 big moments per 30 s on the peak and the importance-3 beats, and plans the seams. Variety quotas are gone; only a one-trick film warns. Every field, the format and the seam pass are in `references/score.md`; the score also declares one `blur_grammar` and exports `curves.json` and `hits` for the sound (`references/score.md`). **When `story/moves.json` exists it binds the score**: the carrier is the spine and the motif, every hero move becomes a shot or a seam built around its bridge frame, each hero is accounted for in `score.moves` (`used` with scene and seam, or `dropped` with a reason), and every continuity seam carries a `bridge` (one sentence: the frame where both scenes are true; `crew.mjs check` errors on a technique label there). Reference moments are the mechanics the moves are executed with where they fit; they never replace a hero move. It writes `$RUN/motion/score.json` + `score.md`: the spine and the motif, the energy curve, the depth plan (which scenes are `2d`, `3d` or `hybrid`, and for each 3D scene its lens, camera legs, light and materials: `references/3d.md`), every scene as a time-coded shot sequence (primary mover, entrances by the object's nature, camera tier, the product moving like the product), every seam as a designed pair with handoff numbers on both sides (including the 2D ↔ 3D continuities `flat-to-depth`, `depth-to-flat`, `camera-through`), the one signature, and the sound events. Dramatic or character scenes may carry timed `reads[]` (notice, action, reaction, payoff; `references/reads.md`): `crew.mjs check` runs `scripts/reads.mjs check` and warns, and `reads.mjs sheet` makes the four-position review sheet for the critic. Accept it on `crew.mjs check --role motion-director --key score`. Push `transitions` and `motion` done from it ("Cuts and three shared-element seams, one push-through at the reveal (scene 6 → 7): the report opens out of the source chip").
5. **Boards** (scene routes): already designed, judged and approved before the music and the score (§3d); their PNGs are the animatic's thumbs. **A lyric video draws key frames here** (the frame designers, in parallel: `crew.mjs brief --role frame-designer --key <a>-<b>` for each range the plan lists). Each designs one still per scene: the scene's peak moment from the score, at the film's aspect, in the chosen design system (`$RUN/direction/DIRECTION.md`, the look's `frame.md` with its font loading), with the **real product** (the research's screens and measured UI kit, real content, never grey bars). Each is standalone HTML in `$RUN/frames/<n>.html` (root sized with `data-width`/`data-height`), rendered with `design.mjs stills`. Then the **frames critic** (`--role critic --key frames-1`); high findings go back to their designer once. Look at every PNG yourself before pushing.
5b. **Style-match gate** (branded launch, promo and brand films, rule 19; on a scene route it ran on the boards in §3d): render the stills, then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/brandfilm.mjs compare --ref "$RUN/brand-film/grammar.json" --ours "$RUN/frames"`. Exit 1 = FAIL: re-dispatch the frame designers with the failed checks and re-run until it passes, **before** the frames critic, the animatic push or any animation. Put the numbers in `decisions.style_match.keyframes` and tell the user in one console line ("Matches OpenAI's film style: 76% white canvas vs 77%, palette dE 4.1").
6. **Gate the plan**: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/slop.mjs --project "$RUN/frames" --scenes "$RUN/scenes.json" --run "$RUN" --json` (copy, look and timing tells in the key frames and the scene timing); fix every error before the user sees it.
7. **Push `animatic`**: `scenes: [{id, title, line, visual, duration, thumb: "$RUN/frames/<id>.png"}]`, `music: {title, file: "$RUN/bed.wav", alternatives: [{id, title, mood, file: <its preview>}]}`, `voice: {name, alternatives: [{id, name}]}` (omit when silent).

Answers:
- `comment` → collect. `apply {ids}` → revise only the scenes the notes touch (script, timing, key frame, music fit), `resolve`, re-push with the same scene ids. A note with `scope: "film"` applies to the whole film.
- `swap {chip: "music"|"voice", id}` → refit / re-voice and re-push. `more {what: "angle"}` → a new pitch from the next device (`story.mjs pick --exclude <used>`), a new animatic.
- `approve` → close `animatic`, go to Build.

## 4b · Build (inside the Animatic state; the user watches)

Write `$RUN/video-decisions.json` (format: `references/video.md`; `direction` = `$RUN/direction`; `look` = `{frame, name}` from the picked style, or `{design_md, mode}` for the brand's own; `brand`/`brand_mode`; `music: {path: "$RUN/bed.wav", title}`; `keyframes: [{scene, image: "$RUN/frames/<id>.png"}]`, the approved boards: one entry per score scene, `scene` = its number (1, 2, …) and `image` = the board it names in `board`, so `video.mjs write` copies each to `assets/keyframes/<scene>.png`, where the scene animators and the builder find it; a lyric video lists its key frames the same way) and hand off:

```bash
node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/video.mjs write --project-dir videos/<name> --decisions "$RUN/video-decisions.json"
```

It writes `BRIEF.md` (the workflow asks nothing), `frame.md` (the look + the motion contract + the art direction), `DIRECTION.md`, `motion.md`, the approved `STORYBOARD.md` / `SCRIPT.md`, the music bed and `DISPATCH.md`. A brand font it cannot stage becomes Inter, and that is never silent: `write` logs a downgrade (the drawer's "Working without") and lists it in `downgrades`; tell the user which font and what the film loses, and get the file into `assets/fonts/` when you can. Read `~/.claude/skills/<route>/SKILL.md` and follow it on `videos/<name>` (install if missing: `npx hyperframes skills update <route>`), keeping these in force:

1. **Adopt the plan, and the score is the visual design.** Don't regenerate STORYBOARD.md/SCRIPT.md or re-run `build-frame.mjs`. Right after `video.mjs write`: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/crew.mjs storyboard --run "$RUN" --project videos/<name>` writes the Motion Director's score into STORYBOARD.md in the workflow's visual-design format (`## Video direction`; per frame the shot sequence, blueprint, focal, roles, sfx, handoffs, `transition_in`, and the plan's line, picture, take, UI and exit), checked with the workflow's own parser, and marks that step done in BRIEF.md. **The score is the plan** (`motion/score.json`: film-level `duration`, `brand`, `feature`, `ground`, `ink`, `current`, `brandReveal`; per beat `id`, `start`, `end`, `line`, `picture`, `moment`, `take`, `ui`, `exit`, `carrier`), the one contract: the same command renders MOTION-SCORE.md and BUILD.md (the builder's short brief) from it, marks DIRECTION.md "the plan wins", and on a direct film reduces DISPATCH.md to a pointer. Continue with the audio step, then `stage-assets.mjs`; skip the workflow's own visual-design writing. Pass `--voice <id>` (and `--provider heygen` for product-launch-video) to the audio step. If the workflow draws `storyboard.html`, check it against the approved key frames yourself.
2. **After `frame-packets.mjs`:** `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/video.mjs inject --project-dir videos/<name>`. **A direct film** (`direct: true` in `crew/plan.json`: 45 s or less, or a single-feature launch) is built by **ONE film builder**, who replaces the workflow's frame workers and writes every beat and every carrier in one pass: `crew.mjs brief --run "$RUN" --role film-builder --key film --project videos/<name>` (`agents/film-builder.md`; the prompt carries the plan, BUILD.md, the packets, the look, the real screens, the reference moments, **the approved boards (`frames/<id>.png` and `frames/shots.json`: the visual target; at each beat's peak the film looks like its board)**, and **the Moves pass's `story/moves.json`: its carrier is the one carrier the builder builds, and the hero cards with their roughs are its motion target (beat the rough, don't copy its grey-box look)**). Accept it on `crew.mjs check --role film-builder --key film`, mark every frame `animated`, assemble, then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/video.mjs carriers --project-dir videos/<name>` (mounts the carriers as their own tracks). No seam pass: one hand made every seam, and the motion gate measures them. **A long film** keeps parallel animators: first the **lead builder** (`--role film-builder --key lead`) builds the root and every carrier (the Moves pass's carrier first: `story/moves.json`'s `carrier` and the objects that cross a cut are one idea), then the **scene animators** replace the workflow's frame workers, one per scene, all in parallel: `crew.mjs brief --run "$RUN" --role scene-animator --key <n> --project videos/<name>` (the prompt carries the workflow's technical role and the scene's packet, **the scene's move card and its rough paths when `score.moves` puts a hero in it (the card and the rough are its motion target: beat it, don't copy its grey-box look)**, DISPATCH.md, the scene's approved board (`frames/<id>.png`) as the visual target, the scene's score and seams, and the vocabulary recipes for the techniques it names). Each looks at its own motion with `crew.mjs strip` before handing back; accept each on `crew.mjs check --role scene-animator --key <n>`, and mark the frame `animated` in STORYBOARD.md as the workflow does. **Lyric videos:** each animator also gets `music/lyrics.json`, `music/audio.json`, the plate's lines with their word timings and the style bible, and syncs every word to its sung start with the music runtime (`RasanMusic.gsapWords` / `RasanMusic.wordProgress`; `references/lyrics.md` has the calls and how the runtime reaches the project); the critics judge the sync from strips across word starts. Scenes the score puts in 3D or hybrid are built with **Rasan3D** (`references/3d.md`; `video.mjs inject` installs the runtime as `assets/three/` when the storyboard has 3D): real three.js on the scene's own timeline, with true motion blur and depth of field, gated by `stage3d.mjs check`. Then assemble, mount the lead's carriers (`video.mjs carriers`), and dispatch the **Motion Director's seam pass** (`--key seams`): every cut checked frame by frame, pops fixed, the signature transition built by one hand.
2b. **The shape kit, key-pose boards and fine review.** Core GSAP moves boxes; the references move shapes. `video.mjs inject` installs `RasanShape` (`assets/rasan-shape/`, `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/shape.mjs install --project videos/<name>`; `shape.mjs check` confirms it when a composition calls it) and the builders use it for path morphs (a letter into a letter, a round pupil into a slit), stroke draw-on (handwriting, connector lines, line art), motion along a path, text splitting and seeded scatter, ink and liquid transitions, pixel, halftone and dither dissolves, squash and stretch, smear frames and stepped timing, all on the paused timeline so every frame is seek-safe (`references/shape.md`). **Before a hero move is built, the builder renders a key-pose board of 4 to 6 stills** (start, anticipation, smear or bridge, settle, end; `design.mjs stills` into `$RUN/boards/<move>.png`) and looks at it; **after the build, every hero move is reviewed as a 24 fps strip** (`crew.mjs strip --fps 24 --from <t0> --to <t1>`), never only a 2 fps sheet. `crew.mjs check --role film-builder` warns `no-pose-board` and `hero-not-stripped`. Showcase grammars (specimen sheet, pixel collage, glyph chain, viewfinder reel, editorial grid) want 5 to 10 independently timed layers at the peak of a shot (`layers-thin`), and the `character` motion language (squash, anticipation, overshoot, smear, stepped; `references/craft.md`, "Animation principles") for object-led films.
2c. **The camera kit and the finish.** The boards were drawn with `RasanCamera` (`runtime/rasan-camera.js`, installed by `video.mjs inject` as `assets/rasan-camera/` whenever a composition references it, together with the GSAP plugins bundle); the builders animate the same rigs: a 3D world from the lens (`stage(root, {lens_mm})`, `place`, camera legs: dolly, truck, orbit, push, rack), depth of field by distance and rack focus, contact shadows and the shadow ladder, glass, the brand-colour rim light, the key light and a tinted living ground, grain, and `blurTrail`/per-layer speed blur so drafts already show motion blur on fast moves (`references/cinematography.md`: 38 recipes with numbers, each from a reference film). Budgeted extras (bloom, halation, chromatic split, light comet, burst) need a `budget` entry in the score; `camera.mjs check` warns on overuse. Review drafts with `finish.mjs --draft` (4-sample blur) so hero moves are judged with blur, not only at delivery.
2d. **The UI rebuild kit.** A beat whose proof is the product is a captured screen rebuilt as DOM in the film's own brand tokens with `RasanUI` (`runtime/rasan-ui.js`, installed by `video.mjs inject` as `assets/rasan-ui/` when a composition names it, or `scripts/uikit.mjs install`): product primitives that cannot draw the slop tells (greeting headers, status-dot label . value cards, placeholder copy), a cursor on its own top layer with press physics, typing only in a real input field, the readability gate (UI text at 0.04 H or more, the hero at 0.55 W or more or cropped; `U.check` warns) and the `data-ui="captured"` marker for faithful rebuilds (`references/ui-rebuild.md`).
3. **Sound (every route, narrated or not, the direct path included):** narrated films are re-timed to the real voiceover by the workflow's sync step: re-run `sound.mjs fit --track <file> --scenes <the synced timeline.json> --reveal <s> --logo <s> --out "$RUN/music/plan.json"` and `sound.mjs render --plan "$RUN/music/plan.json" --out videos/<name>/assets/music-bed.wav` after it (an unnarrated film renders the bed once, from the fit made at the Animatic). After the build (and after the workflow's `fetch-sfx` where it has one), replace its cue list, with events from the builder's `## Events` (every causal event and every transition with its time; on a long film `$RUN/crew/animators/*.md` plus the seams in the score): `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/sound.mjs sfx-plan --scenes <the plan's timeline.json> --plan "$RUN/music/plan.json" --bed videos/<name>/assets/music-bed.wav --events <the "## Events" lines of $RUN/crew/animators/*.md, which are the built frames' events> --words videos/<name>/audio_meta.json --project videos/<name>` and put its `audio_meta_sfx` into `audio_meta.json` `sfx` (causal on-screen events and the score's seams that move, within budget, varied, none where the music already hits, each placed by its measured sync point; a whoosh on a transition through space, a hit when an object lands, a click on a UI tap, a tick on a counter, an impact on the reveal). The direct path never skips this: without it the film has no SFX (`sound.mjs check` errors `no-sfx`). Then `video.mjs audio-lock --project-dir videos/<name> --music videos/<name>/assets/music-bed.wav` (it detects the mastered bed and sets its level under the voice) before `assemble-index`.
4. **Show the build:** push `build` with `scenes` (each `{id, title, duration, thumb, state: todo|working|done, frame}`) `--status working` as each scene starts and finishes (`frame` = a still of the built scene), `latest` = the newest still; `activity` for each step ("Animating scene 3 of 7: the turn, about 2 min left").
5. **Gates** after the workflow's verify step, before its render: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/obey.mjs --project videos/<name>` (the motion contract, per frame), `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/stage3d.mjs check --project videos/<name>` (before a beat sets brand type in 3D, `stage3d.mjs font --project <dir> --family "<Brand Font>" --weight 700` stages the face and `k.extrudeText(text, { font: "<printed path>" })` uses it; every 3D scene: it builds, renders the same pixels for the same time, isn't blank, eases on motion.md's eases, blurs its fast moves, comes to rest, fits a render; exit 0 with `no-3d` when there are none) and `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/slop.mjs --project videos/<name> --run "$RUN"` (the built compositions; includes the logo check: a logo on screen that is not the downloaded file is an error) and `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/motion-gate.mjs --project videos/<name> --plan "$RUN/motion/score.json"` (the code half of the motion gate: carriers, one brand reveal, parked lines). Then render the draft (step 6) and run the motion gate on it before any critic sees the film: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/motion-gate.mjs --video <draft.mp4> --project videos/<name> --plan "$RUN/motion/score.json" --json > "$RUN/crew/motion-gate.json"` (exit 2 = findings, each with its time and a fix: back to the builder, re-render, re-run). Then the **motion and grounding critics** (`--role critic --key motion-1` and `grounding-1`); the motion and film critics never judge without the draft and that report (`crew.mjs check` refuses them). Every finding goes to the film builder (a direct film) or to the animator of its scene (`SendMessage` to that animator while it's addressable, else a fresh dispatch with the finding), leading with the push ("competent isn't the bar: fix these, and show what you can actually do with this scene"), then re-verify. At most 2 rounds (`motion-2`, …); leftovers go to the Final as fix-or-waive (`obey.mjs waive …`). **Every fix round after the first draft is A/B'd before it is accepted** (`agents/ab-juror.md`): fixing to green is how a film gets safer, and the gates cannot see it. Keep the previous version's render, then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/crew.mjs ab --run "$RUN" --a <previous render> --b <this round's render> --plan "$RUN/motion/score.json"` shuffles the two into X and Y (seeded; the order goes in `crew/ab-<k>.json`, never in the brief), draws them side by side at the same seconds with both endings and a phone sheet each, and writes the brief for the **A/B juror**, a motion designer who is never told which is newer and picks the one he would show (boldness, readability, ending, rhythm). Dispatch it, then `crew.mjs check --run "$RUN" --role ab-juror --key <k>` maps the pick back and prints `keep` (the new version won: accept the round) or `revert` (it lost). **A lost round is reverted**: back to the previous version's project state (`git checkout` of the project, or its `.superseded` copy), keeping only the fixes that do not lose (re-apply one at a time and A/B again when unsure), and logged as a decision with `--because "round N lost the blind A/B: <loses_on>"`. Borrow what `loses_on` says the loser did better. Exit 1 = could not run: report it, never call it clean.
6. **Draft render** (never start two renders by hand at once: go through `finish.mjs`, `reel.mjs` or `node scripts/lib/slot.mjs run --label x -- <cmd>`, which take a render slot; run it in the background if you like, but **do not end your turn while it, or any crew member or check you started, is still running**: rule 18; poll the render and `crew.mjs status`, keep posting `activity`, and read each result before moving on) at the workflow's draft quality, then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/sound.mjs check --video <draft.mp4> --film <seconds> --sfx videos/<name>/index.html --plan "$RUN/music/plan.json"` (loudness, true peak, silent or quiet openings, abrupt endings, audible loops, music under the voice, SFX density, `no-sfx`, a flat bed, a ring-out ending; add `--silent-by-choice "<reason>"` only when the brief wants silence) `slop.mjs --project videos/<name> --video <draft.mp4>` (dead air, black open) and the motion gate on the draft (above; it writes `$RUN/crew/motion-gate.json`): fix and re-render on exit 2; loudness alone → `sound.mjs master` (fixes the mix without touching the picture). **Style-match gate on the draft** (branded films, rule 19): `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/brandfilm.mjs compare --ref "$RUN/brand-film/grammar.json" --ours <draft.mp4> [--film <name> | --structure one-take]` (the cut rate and tempo are the brand film's: when the script follows a different film than the first one measured, e.g. a one-take product spot rather than a 110 s brand film, `measure --film <name> --structure <type>` each film into grammar.json and name it here; a one-take film with no film of its kind to follow gets cut rate as a warning); on FAIL fix the failing scenes (canvas, colour, cut rate) and re-render before polish; record `decisions.style_match.draft` and push the short note. **The frame gate on the draft**: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/frame-gate.mjs --video <draft.mp4> --project videos/<name> --plan "$RUN/motion/score.json"` (add `--brand-film "$RUN/brand-film/grammar.json"` on a branded film); always pass `--project`: without it the DOM checks (competing heroes, text load, giant moments) are skipped, and the gate says so. Its `boldness` check asks where the big moments sit: with `story/intent.json` they belong in the peak beat and half the importance-3 beats (`boldness-placement`, a warning); without one the floor is a quarter of the frames (an error; a declared stillness style warns). Never just a bigger font everywhere. The motion gate adds the emphasis curve (`emphasis-off-peak`, `emphasis-on-minor`) and the big-moment budget (`budget-*`); `--no-emphasis` skips it, `--no-guards` skips its white-flash, seam-blank and hold-frame-drift warnings. **The boards against the draft** (scene routes): `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/frames.mjs match --run "$RUN" --video <draft.mp4>` renders the draft at each board's peak next to its board (`frames/match.png`, SSIM on a 160x90 grey downscale in `frames/match.json`); look at it, and the motion and film critics get it: a beat that has drifted from its approved picture (another hero, a lost crop, a flat ground, small type) goes back to its builder. Then the **film critic** (`--role critic --key film-1`) on the draft, which writes a blind retell from the phone sheet before opening `intent.json` (a mismatch with the proposition is high; in round 1 of the frames and film lenses a critic may return structure findings, each an `edit` (cut, merge, move, peak, rest, rewrite) that `crew.mjs check` hands back as `structure_edits` and the Director applies with `lib/intent.mjs` `applyEdits`, then re-times with `scenes.mjs`; from round 2 the structure is locked): its scores go in the Final's `context`, its findings are fixed (one round) or offered as fix-or-waive.

### New creative policy (plan has `creative_policy_revision: 1`)

- Build waits for the user's Storyboard approval (the animatic call: a consumed `approve`, or an authorized "Just make it"); then `crew.mjs creative revise --run "$RUN" --mode build`. The Motion Director writes `creative/execution-map.json` (each signature event to score shot ids, its board and source files); builders add `produced_artifact_refs` and declare deviations; `crew.mjs check` verifies the map. A matching map proves traceability, not that the event is visible: the film critic judges the render.
- The packet carries the creative intent, the event invariants and the approved boards as mandatory sections. Record the harness's image reads with `crew.mjs check ... --observed-reads <paths>`: what was sent is not what was understood. The universal craft quotas of the Moves pass (an atom list, a bridge per boundary, a full-frame hero, chain density) are profiles on this path: `moves-lib` warns unless the profile is selected.
