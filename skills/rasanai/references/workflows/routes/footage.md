## Footage reels (route `reel`)

Brief → **Footage** → Story → Look → **Cut** (the reel's Animatic) → Build → Final. RasanAI's `reel.mjs` examines the footage, builds the cut as a HyperFrames composition (segments, Claude's cards and overlays, captions from the transcript, the music bed ducked under speech) and HyperFrames renders it. Formats: `references/reel.md`.

1. **Footage**: **Transcripts are a promise, not a best effort.** Before the scan run `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/transcriber.mjs status` (JSON; exit 0 = a working whisper-cli + model, proven on a silent wav). If it is not ready, ask the user once (console question: install the transcriber, about 466 MB, or continue without transcripts). **Yes** = run `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/transcriber.mjs install` (in the background; poll; it downloads the model, installs or builds whisper-cli and proves it). Success = re-run the scan, which then transcribes. Failure (exit 3) = show the user its `reason` in plain words and ask again: retry, or continue without transcripts. **After a yes you never continue without transcripts, never fall back to loudness or shot-change guesses, and never write "no transcriber could be installed" in an activity line on your own**: only the user's explicit "continue without" lets you pass `--no-transcribe`, and then you say so on the Footage page. `reel.mjs scan` exits 3 with "TRANSCRIBER NOT READY" when the transcriber is missing, and that is a stop, not a warning. Then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/reel.mjs scan --footage "<folder>" --run "$RUN" [--lang <code>]` probes every clip, draws contact sheets, finds shot changes and silences, and transcribes speech word by word. **Look at every contact sheet** and read the transcripts. Push `footage` with `clips`; the user unticks clips to leave out (`submit {include:[ids]}`).
2. **Story, then Look**: three cuts as scripts (what the reel is for, its shape from hook to payoff, the card and overlay lines), written from what the footage actually contains; then three card and overlay design systems for the picked cut.
3. **Cut**: draft `$RUN/reel.json`: segments on word boundaries (in = a word's start − 0.1 s, out = its end + 0.15 s), shot changes as cut points, the hook in the first 2 s; cards between clips; overlays for names and callouts; `captions.on` for speech; `mute: true` on b-roll that fights the music; `fit` cover/contain/blur; music fitted with `sound.mjs fit` as above. Push `reel` with `timeline`, `overlays`, `captions`, `clips`, `edl` and a draft `video`; the user edits in/out points and cards there (`submit` → write back) or leaves notes.

   For speech, before drafting: run `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/footage.mjs retakes --run "$RUN"` (candidates on word ids: retakes, false starts, "wait / let me start again", fillers, long pauses, alternate hooks; um/uh are pre-accepted and restorable). Decide each `check` candidate from the story (keep a line said twice on purpose; cut the weaker take, normally the earlier one) with `footage.mjs cut --clip <id> --op accept|reject --ids … --by agent --note "<why>"`, then `footage.mjs cut-plan --clip <id> --check-splices` and put `{"type": "cut", "cut": "footage/cuts/<id>.cut.json"}` in the timeline instead of hand-typed in/out points. Anchor name and callout overlays to words (`"anchor": {"cut": …, "word": "<id>"}`), never to guessed seconds. Push `reel` with `cut` from `footage.mjs cut-payload` as well as the timeline. A `cut-edit` action from the user is applied with `footage.mjs cut --action '<its value>' --by user`, then re-run `cut-payload` and push again; it is not one of the five calls and never ends the Cut step by itself. With references: `research.mjs profile --refs <files> --run "$RUN"` once, then `motion-md.mjs write … --reference-profile "$RUN/reference/profile.json"` (add `--brand-locked` on branded films: the brand's grammar wins) and `"reference_profile"` in reel.json; `research.mjs profile-compare` on the draft is a warning, read it to the user.
4. **Build**: `reel.mjs briefs --reel "$RUN/reel.json" --project-dir videos/<name>` writes a brief per card and overlay: design and animate each from the direction: one scene animator per card for several (`crew.mjs brief --role scene-animator`, with the card brief as its packet), each looking at its motion with `crew.mjs strip`. Then `reel.mjs build --reel "$RUN/reel.json" --project-dir videos/<name> --render --quality draft` (lint + obey; refuses while a card is missing). Read every warning to the user.
5. **Final**: as above; on approval rebuild with `--quality delivery`.

A bespoke card (a product animation, a logo sting) can be built through **Single units** and dropped into the cut as `{"type": "card", "src": "videos/<unit>/compositions/index.html", "duration": N}`.
