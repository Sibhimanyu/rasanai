## Presenter films (route `presenter`)

Brief → **Footage** → Story → Look → Animatic → Build → Final. A person talking to camera, shot on a green or blue screen (or any single-subject talking clip to be put "into" other worlds), is keyed out; behind and around them the film cuts between generated image plates made for what they are saying, with camera moves and motion graphics. The voice is the spine and the clip plays whole. Playbook, plan format and checks: `references/presenter.md`; prompts: `references/imagery.md`; scripts: `presenter.mjs`, `imagegen.mjs`.

0. **The owner's answers.** If the request has PREFERENCES (or `rasanai-brief.json` has `preferences`), they decide the taste calls below; see "The owner's answers and series". Pass the brief file to every presenter command as `--prefs <rasanai-brief.json>` (`crew.mjs plan --prefs` records it, and the crew's check commands carry it).
1. **Footage**: `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/reel.mjs scan --footage "<clip folder>" --run "$RUN"` (transcript and contact sheet; copy the clip's word transcript from `footage.json` to `$RUN/presenter/transcript.json`), then `node $SKILL_DIR/scripts/presenter.mjs key --clip <clip> --out "$RUN/presenter"` (it also measures the marks already in the footage: a badge, a logo, a watermark, into `key.json.marks[]`; `presenter.mjs marks --clip <clip> --out "$RUN/presenter"` re-runs just that) and `presenter.mjs beats --transcript "$RUN/presenter/transcript.json" --out "$RUN/presenter/beats.json"`. **Look at `key-check.png`** (the edge over grey and over magenta) and at the contact sheet; a poor key is a `warnings` line to the user, not something to hide. Push `footage`.
2. **Story**: three **visual writers** (`crew.mjs plan --route presenter [--prefs <rasanai-brief.json>] …`, `crew.mjs brief --role visual-writer --key Sure|Bold|Wild`) each write `presenter/plan-<Label>.json`, accepted on `crew.mjs check` (which runs `presenter.mjs check`). Push `story` with each plan as beats: `on_screen` = the graphics' text, `vo` = `say`, `visual` = the layout and the plate prompt in plain words; the title and `idea` are the logline. `choose` copies the plan to `presenter/plan.json`. When `pictures` is answered the three writers write three variations inside that style, not the Sure / Bold / Wild ladder (the labels stay as the card names); in a series there is one writer and the story is auto-chosen.
3. **Look**: the design desk as usual; every DESIGN.md also carries an `## Imagery` section (`design.mjs check-system` requires it on this route).
4. **Animatic**: `presenter.mjs plates --plan presenter/plan.json --look look/DESIGN.md --out "$RUN/presenter/images.json"` writes the batch; run `imagegen.mjs batch --plan … ` **in the background** and post an `activity` line per image, saying the count first ("Making 9 pictures for the film: about 5 minutes"; each image is a Codex run of about 1.5 minutes on the user's ChatGPT plan). Look at `imagegen.mjs sheet` yourself and regenerate weak plates with a sharpened prompt, then `presenter.mjs stills` and push `animatic` with the stills as the scenes and the clip's audio as `music.file`. A note on a plate gets a new prompt and only that plate is regenerated. A `real` plate (a sourced picture of the actual thing) is never generated: `presenter.mjs plates` lists it with its `source`, you save the image to `$RUN/presenter/plates/<id>.jpg` (from the source URL, or the owner's files), and when it cannot be found the plan carries a `todo` and you ask for the file with `console.mjs ask` (a question, not a call).
5. **Build**: `presenter.mjs build --plan presenter/plan.json --key presenter/key.json --plates "$RUN/presenter/plates" --project-dir videos/<name> [--captions <words.json>] --prefs <rasanai-brief.json> --look look/DESIGN.md` (`--captions` only when captions are not answered off: `plain` and `styled` use reel.mjs's `clean` and `bold` styles; `marks=keep` lays the marks over the film from the original clip; `ending=card|make` adds the ending beat after the last word and the film is that much longer) writes the project, its graphic scaffolds and `briefs/graphics/`; one scene animator per graphic (`crew.mjs brief --role scene-animator --key b3-1 --project videos/<name>`) and per designed plate (`--key plate-p2`, from `briefs/plates/`); then the gates (`obey.mjs`, `slop.mjs`), a draft render, the critics.
6. **Final** as above.

**Without Codex** (`IMAGEGEN` not `ready`): the visual writers use `designed` plates (Claude-drawn backdrops in the design system), the console says so once with how to turn images on (`npm install -g @openai/codex`, then `codex login`), and everything else is the same.
