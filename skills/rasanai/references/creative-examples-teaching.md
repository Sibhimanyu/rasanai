# Creative examples: teach decisions, do not hard-code a style

Loaded only by roles that make or judge the creative idea on a new-policy run (`crew/plan.json` has `creative_policy_revision: 1`): the script writers and editor, the concept critic, the styleframe designer, the frame juror, the Motion Director and the critics. Builders get their slice of the idea from the packet instead. Four small original working examples sit in `library/creative-examples/` (HTML, unreviewed originals: see its `PROVENANCE.json`); they are teaching material, never evidence of professional parity.

These are original proposals for the product category in the failed draft. They are not existing advertisements, claims that the app supports every interaction, or instructions to skip the user's Story/Look choices. The facts and actual UI must be verified from the user's permitted product source. They illustrate the specificity expected from RasanAI.

## 1. Three different Story proposals for an English-learning app

### A. "The next sentence is yours"

**Premise:** Help is useful when it changes what you can do next. Follow one small correction into a later practice/use moment.

**Proof order:** a real error -> correction plus a comprehensible reason -> a supported practice opportunity -> the same principle used again -> clear product identity/next action.

**Why this is a story:** the later result depends on the earlier event. It is not a feature catalog. It requires evidence for the practice/reuse behavior; otherwise choose another premise.

**Conceptual visual event:** the relevant phrase is the persistent subject. Its error disappears, its explanation attaches to the changed part, then it returns in a new context with less assistance. No finished camera or material choice is locked at Story.

### B. "Same words. Two different meanings."

**Premise:** Help makes intended meaning precise, not just grammar cleaner.

**Proof order:** a sentence with two legitimate readings -> show the practical distinction -> the person chooses the intended reading -> a precise rewrite retains the right people/conditions -> product identification.

**Proof boundary:** preserve the source's actual ambiguity. For the bonus example, the alternatives are all managers plus qualifying employees versus qualifying members of both groups. Dropping employees is wrong. Do not use an inaccurate demonstration just because two chips look clean.

**Conceptual visual event:** the shared sentence separates into its two readings; choosing one resolves the structure. Later Look work decides whether this is typographic, diagrammatic or UI-led.

### C. "Start in your own words"

**Premise:** A person can begin with their natural rough phrasing and shape it for different needs.

**Proof order:** raw message -> appropriate grammar/tone/vocabulary improvement -> a different context showing breadth -> a follow-up that preserves the person's meaning -> product identity.

**Proof boundary:** not every sentence needs to become shorter, more formal or more intense. Context determines what 'better' means. Do not claim improved long-term learning from one edited sentence.

**What differs:** A proves transfer, B proves precision, C proves usable breadth. These are different communication ideas, not the same six cards with different titles.

## 2. Three Look options for ONE approved beat

Assume Story A was selected and the representative beat is a correction plus its reason. Keep exact source, correction and required explanation identical in all three options.

| Look | Visual language | What changes meaningfully | What remains fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial language | Oversized but readable language; white space; a small authentic UI anchor; deliberate typographic transformations. | The sentence, rather than its panel, is the focal image. | Real words, source behavior, brand type/palette. |
| Graphic explanation | Flat annotated relationships, a clear before/change/after; purposeful color semantics. | The viewer follows cause and effect instead of decorative depth. | Same correction and explanation; no invented learning capability. |
| Tactile product | Carefully composed close product fragment; depth/light only where chosen; a physically coherent movement. | Material and spatial emphasis support the proof. | Same words/behavior and authentic UI; no generic glass-card pack. |

Use the existing Look cards. Do not create a sixth user choice called Creative Direction. A brand that excludes a medium excludes it here; do not force three impossible options merely to fill slots.

## 3. A signature event developed only AFTER Look is chosen

For Story A plus Editorial language:

**Before:** a legible source phrase contains 'discuss about'. The product identity/context is clear enough to establish where the correction comes from.

**Change:** the specific unnecessary word separates from the phrase. The explanation is attached to that grammatical relation, not placed as an unrelated slogan in the corner. The correct phrase remains readable at rest.

**After:** a later supported exercise or conversation uses 'discuss' correctly. Reduce the visual assistance so the relationship reads as progress, but never claim the person learned if the film only shows an edited answer. The narrative payoff must match the proof that exists.

**Sound:** a small decisive event and a moment of space can make the change land. No compulsory whoosh. The exact mix follows existing audio direction.

**Do not substitute:** a card labeled 'Practice' is not the later use; a random particle flourish is not the grammatical relationship; a chart is not proof of retention.

The implementation can be custom DOM/SVG/GSAP using existing deterministic tools. No external renderer is needed to try this concept. All actual trials still wait for the user's test approval.

## 4. Negative example: clean is not necessarily directed

The supplied montage shows repeated headline-plus-card frames. The problem is not the existence of cards, white space or restrained motion. The problem is that different ideas are staged almost identically while the important changes remain subordinate to the headings.

**Bad repair:** tilt every card, add particle transitions, increase type scale on a schedule, insert a dramatic third-act slogan.

**Better repair:** choose the strongest proposition, make the important product event the focal shot, develop it across beats, vary composition when meaning changes, and preserve enough context/reading time for the evidence to be understood.

**Also valid:** the user deliberately wants a calm same-framing before/after comparison. That repeated arrangement is then purposeful; do not reject it using a generic uniqueness quota.

## 5. Specific repair examples for critics

| Weak note | Actionable note |
|---|---|
| Make this more premium. | The correction is too small relative to the headline. Reframe the approved phrase as the focal object while retaining readable explanation and the product cue. |
| Add a creative transition. | This is a causal handoff: the corrected phrase reappears in practice. Carry that exact phrase into the next state, or use a clear cut that preserves the relationship. |
| More energy. | We spend time typing an already established interaction. Enter this beat nearer the new decision and use the saved time for the changed meaning to settle. |
| Looks generic. | Shots 2-5 repeat headline, centered panel and small text-change in the same positions. Identify which beat requires comparison, which requires an isolated result, and which requires continuity; design those relationships rather than recoloring the panel. |
| The ending needs a wow. | The final claim is adaptation, but only bars rise. Show the actual changed exercise if supported, or end on the demonstrated correction-to-practice loop instead. |

## 6. Product standards that cannot be gamed by metadata

A new shot-size label does not create a new composition. A `signature_event` field does not mean the event occurred. A passing schema does not mean the reviewer watched the motion. A screenshot of a UI does not necessarily prove the shown interaction. A cached title card cannot replace the selected visual idea simply because it renders faster.

Preserve specific meaning and user choice while aiming for a result worth remembering. Novelty that harms comprehension or truth is a regression, not a creative victory.

## 7. Mechanisms: take the mechanic, not the demo

A mechanism packet entry (`mechanismPacketSection`) answers one question: what must the viewer perceive, in which phases, implemented how, with what limits. Example: a spotlight sample teaches one shared camera transform over related layers and explicit phase boundaries with a destination hold. It does not teach its palette, its hero card or its story. Use the mechanic in the film's own composition.

Keep the neighbourhood: what prepared the event, what relationship crosses the boundary, how long the result is held. An isolated effect clip can hide the directing decision that made it work.

If the entry is `reference-only`, `unknown-method` or has an unavailable dependency, say so and build a supported original mechanic; do not claim a port. If the person explicitly asks for an authorized template, reuse it deliberately. A diagram whose nodes all move but whose claimed relationship is not visible fails on meaning, not on amount of motion.
