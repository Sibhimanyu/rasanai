## What it is
Muji (無印良品, "No Brand Quality Goods") began in December 1980 as a product label of the Seiyu supermarket chain with the slogan "Lower priced for a reason"; the first dedicated store opened in Aoyama, Tokyo in 1983 and the first overseas store in London in 1991 (Wikipedia). Ikko Tanaka, first art director, developed the identity with marketing consultant Kazuko Koike and interior designer Takashi Sugimoto, including the brown paper and clear cellophane packaging. Kenya Hara became art director in 2001, saying the company "was at a standstill", and re-grounded it in emptiness. His "Horizon" poster won the Tokyo Art Directors Club grand prize in 2003 (Wikipedia, Kenya Hara).

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- Packaging as restraint: brown recycled paper and clear cellophane, minimal functional information (Wikipedia).
- Plain, human copy: the 1980 debut ad "Lower-priced for a reason"; Tanaka's 25 archive posters (1980 to 2000, shown in London and Milan 2016) range from text-heavy newspaper ads to minimalist photographs and simple line drawings (It's Nice That).
- Hara's emptiness: "white denotes emptiness ... Yet emptiness doesn't mean nothingness"; it is a vessel that can hold future content (Wikipedia, Kenya Hara, from his book White, 2010). The empty frame is designed to receive the viewer's meaning.
- Horizon (2003 onward, art direction Hara, photography Tamotsu Fujii per search summary): a borderless horizon with one unplanned human presence, a 14-year-old girl who "coincidentally walks into the camera" (People's Graphic Design Archive); other frames use the Uyuni salt flat in Bolivia and Mongolia (search summary, not fetched). No slogan, only the four characters of the logo at the right of the photograph.
- 60 percent or more of every frame carries nothing; one subject, one horizon at 50 to 66 percent of frame height (proposed rule of thumb, not from a guideline).
- 2003 to 2025 corporate ads are now shown by Hara Design Institute as "Nothing, yet Everything" (hara.ndc.co.jp), which is the clearest short statement of the brief.

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames, GSAP): a wide still with a 2 to 3 percent push over 6 s (`scale` 1 to 1.025, `sine.inOut`); text fades in at 800 ms with opacity only, no translate; the horizon drifts 4 px over 6 s. One cut every 5 to 7 s; dissolves of 1200 ms only (craft.md section 4 would call these the quiet end of the set). No music swell, room tone; if there is a sound bed, a single sustained tone. All timings are proposals; the sources describe stillness but give no numbers.
3D (Rasan3D; references/3d.md sections 3 and 5):
- `Rasan3D.stage({ background: "#e8e0d0", environment: "soft", toneMapping: "neutral", fog: { color: "#e8e0d0", near: 6, far: 30 }, post: { grain: 0.015 }, camera: { pos: [[0,[0,1.1,3.2]],[6,[0.08,1.1,3.0],"sine.inOut"]], target: [[0,[0,0.5,0]]], lens: [[0,35]], fstop: 5.6 } })`. The fog the colour of the ground produces the empty horizon.
- Material: `k.material("paper", { color: "#e0d5c2" })` and `clay` or `ceramic` for the single object; no specular accents. `k.ground({ y: 0, color: "#e0d5c2" })`.
- Light: `k.rig("window", { dir: [-1, 0.8, 0.4], shadows: true, shadowSoftness: 12 })`; broad, low contrast, no rim, no bloom.
- Seam: the maroon logo and the one line of copy stay DOM above the canvas (3d.md section 7), so type remains crisp.

## How to instruct a model to build it
"Build a calm product film in the manner of Muji's Horizon posters. Canvas warm paper #f5f2ec, kraft #af8a6c, ink #1c1c1c, logo maroon #7d0018. At least 60 percent of every frame is empty. One wide still composition with one horizon at 60 percent height; one small object or none. A small maroon logo at the bottom right, 3.5 percent of frame width. One line of copy in Noto Sans JP 400 at 2.6 percent of short side, tracking 0.04em, fading in over 800 ms sine.inOut, no movement. A 2.5 percent push over 6 s sine.inOut. Dissolves 1200 ms. No gradients, shadows, glow, slogan or stock minimal imagery." Claude tends to fill a void with explanation: say what must stay empty. GPT-style output tends to pure white and cold grey: require warm paper and kraft (observed tendencies, not tested here).

## Blending notes
Carries: emptiness as content, warm paper, tiny logo, long reads with one slow mover. Blends with Braun (functional honesty), NHK (observation of objects), Tanaka's Japanese modern and Swiss structure. Do not blend with kinetic or maximal systems; use as a quiet scene inside a larger blend rather than a base.
