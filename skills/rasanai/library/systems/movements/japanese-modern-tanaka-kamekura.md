## What it is
Postwar Japanese graphic design fused modernism with Japanese visual tradition. Yusaku Kamekura (1915-1997), nicknamed "Boss", studied at the Institute of New Architecture and Industrial Arts (1935-1937), where Bauhaus principles reached Japan; he co-founded the Japan Advertising Artists Club (1951) and the Nippon Design Center (1960), and made the 1964 Tokyo Olympics emblem (gold rings under a red circle meant as the sun) and a poster of sprinters using photography that was technically difficult at the time. Ikko Tanaka (1930-2002) worked with "bold and simple geometries in novel arrangements, and vivid swaths of color", drawing on Edo woodblock prints and the Rinpa school; clients included Seibu, Mazda, Issey Miyake and Expo '70, and he art-directed Muji's early identity (kraft paper, clear cellophane, a reddish-brown logo).

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- One idea per sheet: a single dominant geometric form or one photograph, with the rest empty.
- Negative space (ma) is active: the empty area balances the shape off-centre.
- Colour is flat, opaque and few: white or cream, black, and one vivid colour; red as the sun (Kamekura), vivid swaths (Tanaka), kraft brown (Muji).
- Geometry from tradition and Bauhaus: circle, band, grid of squares; Rinpa-like pattern reduced to shape.
- Type is architecture: big kana or Latin as a mass, small supporting text in a quiet corner.
- Photography is exact: high-speed, high-contrast, cropped hard.
- Restraint as a brand: Muji's "no brand" approach and kraft/cellophane packaging; the system carries meaning without decoration.
- Dialogue between tradition and modernity is kept in the structure, not in clipart.

## Tokens decoded
- Colour: measured from a Tanaka Noh poster photograph: off-white `#edecea`, black `#0d0c0e`, vermilion `#b74835`, indigo `#335478` and `#466a86`. Proposed: sun red `#d7141a`, gold `#c9a227`, kraft `#b08a5a`, swath pink `#e8467c`. Use one vivid colour per scene.
- Type: Latin in Jost 500 or Inter Tight; Japanese in Zen Kaku Gothic New 700 or Noto Sans JP 900 (variable `wght`) for mass, Shippori Mincho or Noto Serif JP for refined text. Latin faces do not contain kana, so set Japanese strings explicitly in a JP family and keep `font-family` fallbacks in that order. All OFL.
- Grid: 6 columns, 8 px baseline, margins 120 px; the main shape's centre at roughly 0.4 of frame width (left of centre), text column at the right edge 20% wide.
- Shape scale: a circle of 0.45 x the short side (the sun); bands of 8-12% frame height; no strokes.
- Spacing: the empty area at least 50% of the frame.

## Motion and camera
- The sun: a circle scales from 0 to 1 from its centre over 1200 ms `expo.out` (a sharp arrival, long settle), or slides along a horizontal axis 700 ms `power3.inOut`. Hold 1.2 s.
- Swatch logic: colour bands slide in and stop sharply (`expo.out`, 500 ms), stagger 100 ms; the next scene reuses the band as a wipe.
- Text: mask reveal (clip-path inset from 100% to 0% on one axis, 600 ms `power3.out`), no fade, no bounce; vertical kana columns reveal top to bottom.
- Silence: at least one hold of 1.5-2 s with no movement; calm is the signature.
- Transitions: shape continuation (the circle in scene A becomes the dot of a letter in scene B); hard cut for impact.
- Camera 2D: locked; at most a 3% push over 6 s `sine.inOut`.
- 3D reading (Rasan3D; real API, parameters proposed): flat planes at different depths and a paper fold. Planes `k.pxPlane`-style or `PlaneGeometry` in `k.material("unlit",{color:"#b74835"})` (disc via `CircleGeometry(1,96)`) on `k.material("paper",{color:"#edecea"})` sheets at z = 0, 0.15, 0.3; `environment:"none"`, `toneMapping:"none"` so the flat colours stay exact. Fold: two paper planes as children of a hinge group, `hinge.rotation.y = k.prog(t,0.4,1.3,"power2.inOut")*Math.PI/2`. Light `k.rig("top-soft",{key:"#ffffff",fill:"#ffffff",intensity:0.9,shadows:true,shadowSoftness:10})` for a faint contact shadow only. Camera: `lens:[[0,120]]` (near-orthographic), `fstop` unset, a 3% `pos` dolly in 6s `sine.inOut`, `roll:0`. Type as `k.text({text:"無印",font:"Noto Serif JP",size:1.2,weight:700})` after `@font-face` loading, or DOM through `k.pinDom` for crisp kana.

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready brief:
"Japanese modern in the line of Kamekura and Tanaka. Canvas #f6f2e8, ink #111111, one vivid colour per scene (sun red #d7141a, or Tanaka pink #e8467c, or kraft #b08a5a). One dominant flat geometric shape (circle 0.45 x short side, centred about 0.4 of the width), the rest empty (50% of the frame at least). No gradients, no shadows, no strokes. Type: Zen Kaku Gothic New or Noto Sans JP for Japanese mass, Jost for Latin; a small 24px text column at the right edge. Motion: circle scales from its centre 0 to 1 in 1200ms expo.out, text mask-reveals 600ms power3.out, bands slide 500ms expo.out, 100ms stagger; at least one 1.5s hold with no motion; transitions are shape continuations or hard cuts. Camera locked or 3% push over 6s. No cherry blossoms, no brush-script fonts, no waves."
Claude vs GPT: image-prompted models reach for Orientalist clipart; instruct the geometry and the empty space. Verify Japanese font coverage when setting kana, or text renders with fallback glyphs.

## Blending notes
Carries well: one-shape-per-scene discipline, flat colour, ma, mask-reveal text. Blends easily with Swiss (shared grid and restraint, Japanese adds warmth and a curve) and mid-century (palette warmth). With art nouveau, borrow flat colour and woodblock-like contour, drop ornament. Clashes with maximal styles. Be careful with Japan-related content: use native-speaker copy; text accuracy matters more than the style.

## Cheap tells
- A red circle plus a brush-script "Japanese" font; fake-kanji display faces on Latin text.
- Cherry blossom and wave clipart; centred symmetric layouts with no ma (empty-space) tension.
- Gradients where the originals are opaque flat colour; wrong or machine-garbled Japanese copy.
