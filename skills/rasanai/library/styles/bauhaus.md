# Bauhaus geometric

Circles, triangles and rectangles on a strict modular grid, in primaries on cream, each move an integer number of modules on a beat.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

A kinetic poster in the 1919 Bauhaus and Swiss tradition: mechanical, rational, musical. Geometry translates, rotates and scales exactly; every landing is on a grid line and every move on a beat, one action per beat, with rotation pivots alternating between own centre, corner and frame centre.

Not: bouncy or elastic shapes, a gradient or glow look, or an irregular layout.

## Materials

- Cream paper (with slight tooth and print misregistration allowed), a strict module (e.g. 240 px), primaries red, yellow, blue plus black.

## Colour logic

- A limited primary palette: red, yellow, blue, black on cream.

## Type

- Heavy geometric sans set vertically or in Swiss small caps.

## Motion

- Every displacement is an integer multiple of the module; ease-in-out quad or linear, no overshoot (mechanical feel is the feature).
- Beat-driven: elements appear or flip on the beat, one action per beat; phase-offset cascade waves across the grid.

## Camera grammar

- Locked or grid-aligned moves.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. Basic geometric primitives only: circle, triangle, rectangle.
2. A red, yellow, blue, black palette on cream.
3. Everything aligned to a strict module and landing on grid lines.
4. No overshoot: ease-in-out or linear only.
5. Beat-locked modular moves with alternating rotation pivots.

## When to use

- Design, architecture, education and culture subjects; rigorous systems brands; music-locked kinetic posters; a rhythm-led ident.

Avoid when: Warm, organic, emotional stories; brands with their own non-primary palette.
