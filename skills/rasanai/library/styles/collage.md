# Collage and cut-out

Photo cut-outs with white scissor edges and halftone on newsprint, puppet-jointed, stepped at 12 fps.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

A Dada and constructivist cut-up: public-domain portraits and objects cut out with rough white edges, assembled in surreal proportions on newsprint or kraft paper, with a modern editorial finish. Mouths and jaws are separate cuts, hands slap elements on, and motion is deliberately stepped like stop-motion.

Not: clean vector or smooth 3D; also not a tidy moodboard.

## Materials

- Cut-out images with a 2 to 4 px white outline and a halftone filter; newsprint texture; layers: paper, big colour blocks, cut-outs, doodles and tape, grain.
- Puppet-style segmented joints (shoulder, elbow, jaw anchors); mouth opens with two or three swap frames.

## Colour logic

- Newsprint or kraft ground with bold red and black constructivist accents.

## Type

- Bold constructivist diagonal type in red and black, editorial headlines.

## Motion

- Deliberately low frame rate (12 fps, even 6 fps on holds) with plus or minus 2 px hand-placement jitter.
- Transitions are hands slapping elements on, torn-paper strips or a page flip.

## Camera grammar

- Mostly locked; motion lives in the pieces.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. Cut-outs with a rough white scissor edge and halftone texture.
2. Surreal, off-proportion assemblies of photo pieces.
3. Stepped 12 fps (or lower) motion with small random placement jitter.
4. Separate-cut mouths and jaws that flap.
5. A newsprint or kraft ground with grain on top.

## When to use

- Music, culture, street and editorial stories; irreverent or surreal brand moments; mixed-media 'idea explosion' beats.

Avoid when: Corporate-clean, precision or luxury looks, product UI films.
