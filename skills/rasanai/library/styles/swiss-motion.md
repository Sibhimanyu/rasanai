# Swiss motion graphics

International Typographic Style in motion: a modular grid, one typeface, one signal colour, movement as exact as a printing press.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

Pure 2D graphic design animated on a grid: flat paper, black type and bars, a very light grey and one saturated signal colour. Every element snaps to the grid, every move lands on a beat, nothing bounces. Rigour with one exception: the one element that breaks the system becomes the character. Extreme scale contrast: a giant numeral beside tiny technical notes.

Not: a keynote (no gradients, glow or device renders), lyric kinetic type, data visualisation, or a Bauhaus pastiche of primary shapes.

## Materials

- 1920x1080 frame on a 12-column grid with about 96 px margins, 24 px gutters and a 24 px baseline; the frame can act as a spread (programme on the left, artifact on the right).
- Flat surfaces only: page, paper, ink, signal colour, grid lines in light greys; no gradients, shadows, particles or grain.
- Bars are flat rectangles merged and overdrawn 1 px at seams; light-grey elements under black ones give depth without a second colour; a 2D camera matrix for all moves.

## Colour logic

- Paper, ink, greys and exactly one signal colour (red, orange, ultramarine or green) chosen for the topic, never a second.
- Only one thing carries the signal colour and it is the only thing allowed to be inexact; overprint of black type on the signal colour is allowed.

## Type

- One OFL neo-grotesk family in several weights, about -2 percent tracking at 90 px and up, flush left and ragged right, everything on a grid line; giant numerals optically flush left.
- Subtitles are part of the layout at column starts, clip-revealed word by word, with museum-label captions and a 1 px rule.

## Motion

- Two curves only: a precise ease like cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1) for snaps and linear for drawn lines and the exception; no overshoot, spring or bounce.
- Durations are note values; entrances are wipes or clip-reveals, never fades; deletions are knife cuts; ripples ordered by distance.
- The exception moves linearly and unsnapped; a coordinate readout can flip from 'on grid' to 'off grid'.

## Camera grammar

- Locked spread (cause left, effect right), hard pan by one page or column, rotate 90 degrees and stop, pull out to a wall of artifacts, push into one module, grid extends past the page, split frame into equal cells, vertical scroll through a column.
- Only the precise ease and only on beats; transitions are wipes, knife cuts, camera moves and re-flows.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. A visible 12-column grid with everything aligned to it, flush left, ragged right.
2. One typeface and exactly one signal colour.
3. No overshoot, bounce, gradient or shadow anywhere.
4. Entrances are clip-reveals or wipes and deletions are knife cuts.
5. One element deliberately breaks the grid and moves linearly.

## When to use

- System, brand and design-tool launches; rules, specs and programmes; comparisons (spreads); anything whose story is 'order, and the one thing that breaks it'.
- A calm, authoritative film for a rigorous product.

Avoid when: Playful, glossy or emotional stories; films needing depth or finish.
