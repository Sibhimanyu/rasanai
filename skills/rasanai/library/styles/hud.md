# Cyberpunk HUD / FUI

Film-grade fictional UI: cyan wireframe, rotating rings, radar sweep, rolling data, lock-on brackets that flip to alert orange.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

A heads-up-display skin with density and restraint: layered hex grids, concentric rotating rings with ticks, a conic radar sweep with blips, monospace data columns, typewriter status lines, sparkline graphs and a wireframe hologram centrepiece. It builds in hierarchical order, finds a target, locks, and flips to an alert state.

Not: a flat dashboard or a stock 'tech' gradient.

## Materials

- Deep teal-black ground; cyan line-work with an orange/red alert state; global glow, slight chromatic aberration, scanlines, restrained micro-glitches; seeded random data; a Three.js wireframe hologram.

## Colour logic

- Cyan on a teal-black ground, with orange-red reserved for the alert and lock moment.

## Type

- Monospace numerals and small-caps labels; typewriter lines with a cursor.

## Motion

- Wireframes draw on with stroke dash offset, elements staggered by hierarchy; random numbers flick for 3 to 5 frames before landing on the true value; lock brackets shrink from 1.4 to 1.0 with fast-out slow-in.

## Camera grammar

- Mostly locked, with push-ins on the lock target.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. Cyan linework with an orange alert state on a dark teal ground.
2. Lock-on brackets that snap from 1.4x to 1.0x onto a target.
3. A conic radar sweep and concentric rotating ring with ticks.
4. Monospace data columns that roll for 3 to 5 frames then settle.
5. Global glow, scanlines and slight chromatic aberration.

## When to use

- Tech reviews, security, AI-detection and monitoring products, defence and space explainers; an analysis or 'target found' beat.

Avoid when: Warm, human or editorial brands; calm premium launches.
