# Art Deco

Black lacquer, engraved gold line and airbrushed geometry: a 1930s poster that moves, built on a centre axis.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

Gold keylines on warm black, filled with airbrushed hard-edged volumes, organised around one vertical axis. Ornament is the picture: sunbursts are backgrounds, stepped arches are frames, a dial is a progress bar, a bulb marquee is a reveal. Motion is mechanical and symmetrical: things unfold from the centre and light arrives as bulbs switching on one by one.

Not: a gold-foil slideshow, Art Nouveau (no whiplash curves, no flowers), a flat vector poster, or a 60s spy title.

## Materials

- Warm lacquer black ground, never neutral grey-black; gold as a banded metal gradient (dark, light, hot, light, dark) with an animatable sheen.
- The signature stroke: a dark engraved underline, the gold line and a hairline highlight; no black cartoon outlines.
- Volumes use a hard edge plus airbrush with one consistent light direction and a thin gold rim; structural motifs (sunburst, stepped arch, fan, chevrons, dial); real one-point and two-point perspective.

## Colour logic

- Three constants: warm black, gold, ivory.
- One saturated accent reserved for the protagonist, the only large saturated area; a cooler jewel tone may colour secondary surfaces.
- Any single element can take its own colour through the same keyline and glow while the rest stays gold.

## Type

- Display faces with deco proportions for titles, a thin geometric for numbers, a clean humanist sans for subtitles.
- Titles are architecture: a stepped gold title bar over an arch or sunburst, drawn on and swept by a sheen; subtitles unfold from the centre in a lacquer bar with a double gold keyline.

## Motion

- Characters on twos, camera, light and gold draw-on on ones; everything unfolds from the centre (point, hairline, bars open, text rises).
- Gold draw-on follows the music; bulbs ignite with a 1.6x overshoot then a chase; mechanical easing, nothing floats; the centre axis never drifts.

## Camera grammar

- Symmetric split like door leaves, leaves sliding shut, extreme low-angle tilt, architectural cutaway pull-out, Busby Berkeley mirrored overhead, push-in on a dial, shape match cuts, low oblique along a marquee, pull-back from detail to skyline.
- The centre axis is sacred; transitions are splits, revolves, arch openings and shape matches, never a generic dissolve.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. Gold keylines with the engraved underline-and-highlight stroke on warm black.
2. Everything is composed on a centre vertical axis and unfolds symmetrically from it.
3. Airbrushed hard-edged volumes with one light direction and a gold rim.
4. Bulb marquees light one by one with an overshoot flash.
5. Exactly one saturated accent on the protagonist.

## When to use

- Luxury, hospitality, finance, heritage and celebration films; a grand-opening or awards feel; stories about milestones, counts and deadlines (dials and medallions).
- Brand films wanting ornament as structure.

Avoid when: Minimal developer tools, flat UI demos, anything modern-casual.
