# Shape morph chain

One hero shape continuously becomes six to eight meaningful icons, on the beat, through simple intermediate forms.

A reference for the design desk to blend one or two traits from, never a preset that replaces the film's bespoke system. Never cite it as the whole look; the signature features below are what a blend must keep if it names this style.

## Essence, and what it is not

The core grammar of visual storytelling: a cup becomes a sun, a sunset, a gull, a paper plane, a pin, a heart, a logo. Contours never jump or knot; each A to B passes through a circle or capsule with rotation and squash, and sub-parts morph in sync. Background colour fields change with each morph.

Not: cuts between icons, crossfades, or a morph that tangles mid-way.

## Materials

- SVG paths with matched vertex counts and first-vertex alignment (or a library with optimal vertex matching) and colour-field wipes behind.

## Colour logic

- Clean colour blocks that change per morph; a limited palette with strong contrast.

## Type

- Minimal; the shapes carry the story, ending on an invented wordmark.

## Motion

- A to circle to B in two 8 to 12 frame stages with 10 to 15 percent squash and slight rotation; fastest mid-way (about cubic-bezier(0.7,0,0.3,1)) with 3-frame cushions at each end.
- Check every morph's mid-point still for knotting.

## Camera grammar

- Locked; the transformation is the movement.

## Signature features

Checkable traits; the frame juror and the critic look for each by name when a system claims this style.

1. Each shape flows into the next with continuous outlines and no jump cuts.
2. Complex changes route through a simple intermediate (circle or capsule).
3. Rotation plus 10 to 15 percent squash and stretch during the morph.
4. Sub-parts morph in sync (steam to rays to waves).
5. A background colour field changes with each morph, on the beat.

## When to use

- A chain of capabilities or a journey ('from this to that'); icon-led explainers; app feature sequences; a brand lockup ending.

Avoid when: Photographic, realist or data-dense stories.
